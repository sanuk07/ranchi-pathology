About Ranchi Pathology

Dedicated to Diagnostic Excellence

Founded to bridge the healthcare gap in Jharkhand, Ranchi Pathology delivers accredited laboratory testing, clinical accuracy, and patient-first home sample collections across Chandwa, Lohardaga, and Ranchi.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-accuracy diagnostic testing and compassionate health screening to every family across Jharkhand. We combine automated medical technologies with certified medical oversight to ensure rapid, dependable results that physicians and patients can rely on for critical health decisions.

Our Vision

To be the premier, most reliable pathology and wellness diagnostic network in Eastern India—celebrated for clinical integrity, modern digital delivery, transparent pricing, and effortless doorstep sample collection services in both urban centers and surrounding communities.

Our Clinical Quality Commitment

We maintain zero-compromise standards across every step of your diagnostic journey.

Automated Laboratory Analyzers

Fully automated 5-part hematology counters, dry-chemistry biochemistry instruments, and chemiluminescence analyzers minimizing human variance.

Barcoded Sample Tracking

Samples are barcoded immediately at collection with patient-specific identifiers to eliminate sample mix-ups throughout handling and archiving.

Cold-Chain Home Transport

Home-collected blood and fluid samples are transported in insulated, temperature-monitored carriers to preserve biochemical stability.

Doctor-Verified Reports

All abnormal or critical findings receive manual review and clinical correlation by experienced medical pathologists prior to sign-off.
