Fasting & Pre-Test Guidance

For tests requiring fasting (e.g. Lipid Profile, Fasting Blood Sugar, LFT):

  • Maintain 8–12 hours of overnight fasting.
  • Plain water is permitted unless contraindicated.
  • Take routine prescribed medications unless advised by your doctor.
