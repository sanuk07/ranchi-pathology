Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you book tests, request home collection, or access diagnostic reports through Ranchi Pathology, we collect necessary personal and clinical information:

  • Patient Demographics: Full name, age, date of birth, gender, contact phone number, email address, and home collection address with landmark and pincode.
  • Clinical & Medical Details: Referring physician’s name, test requisitions, prescription copies (if uploaded), clinical notes, and sample collection metadata.
  • Diagnostic Specimen & Test Data: Barcode identifiers, physiological specimen types (blood, urine, tissue), biochemical analyzer readings, and verified pathologist reports.
  • Payment & Transaction Information: Booking reference IDs, UPI payment timestamps, transaction IDs, and receipt status. We do not store sensitive bank account credentials or debit/credit card CVV numbers.
  • Digital Technical Data: IP address, device operating system, browser type, and tracking token cookies used solely for session management and report authentication.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your medical and personal data is utilized strictly for healthcare delivery, patient service, and statutory compliance:

  • Diagnostic Testing & Reporting: Conducting laboratory analyses, generating verified test reports, and enabling clinical review by our medical pathologists.
  • Appointment Fulfillment: Scheduling branch visits, routing certified phlebotomists for home sample collection, and confirming slot availability.
  • Patient Communications: Sending booking confirmations, phlebotomist arrival updates, and automated notifications when your PDF report is ready for download.
  • Report Delivery: Facilitating secure online report download via our authenticated patient portal and delivery to your authorized WhatsApp number.
  • Quality Improvement & Safety: Calibrating automated laboratory instruments, conducting internal quality control audits, and investigating patient feedback.

3. Patient Confidentiality & Data Security

We treat all patient data as privileged health records subject to strict clinical confidentiality:

  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All data transmitted between your browser and our Cloudflare-powered network is encrypted using TLS 1.3 encryption. Stored records and report PDFs are protected with encrypted access controls.
  • Identity Verification for Reports: Reports can only be viewed or downloaded by presenting a valid, cryptographically unique tracking token or by two-factor verification matching the Booking ID with the registered patient phone number.
  • Restricted Staff Access: Access to patient results is limited exclusively to authorized pathologists, laboratory scientists, and attending technicians directly involved in sample processing.
  • Physical Security: Sample processing areas across our Chandwa, Lohardaga, and Ranchi centers are restricted to authorized personnel with strict bio-waste and specimen disposal protocols.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosure

Ranchi Pathology does not sell, rent, or commercialize your personal or health data to pharmaceutical companies, advertisers, or third-party marketing brokers. Disclosures are made only under strict constraints:

  • Specialized Reference Laboratories: For rare or esoteric genomic/histopathology tests not processed in-house, specimens may be referred to accredited NABL partner reference laboratories under reciprocal non-disclosure agreements.
  • Infrastructure & Delivery Partners: Trusted enterprise service providers (Cloudflare Worker storage and Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API) process communication and hosting under strict business associate data security obligations.
  • Statutory & Public Health Mandates: Where required by Indian public health laws (such as notifiable infectious diseases like Dengue, Malaria, or Tuberculosis), reports are reported to designated municipal or state health authorities as mandated by law.

5. Your Rights as a Patient

You have full autonomy over your diagnostic data:

  • Right to Access: You may view, download, and request duplicate copies of your verified diagnostic reports at any time via the patient portal or branch helpdesk.
  • Right to Rectification: If personal information (such as age, spelling of your name, or contact number) was incorrectly captured, you may request prompt correction before final report dispatch.
  • Right to Confidential Communication: You may specify your preferred communication channels (e.g., portal-only download rather than automated WhatsApp alerts).
  • Right to Clinical Explanation: You have the right to request clarification regarding reference ranges or report remarks from our consulting pathology staff.

6. Medical Record Retention

In accordance with Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) diagnostic guidelines and clinical establishment rules:

  • Digital test reports and biochemical result logs are retained for a minimum statutory period of three (3) to five (5) years to support ongoing medical history inquiries.
  • Biological specimens (blood tubes, urine aliquots) are preserved for laboratory re-check windows (typically 24 to 72 hours post-analysis) before being incinerated per biomedical waste management standards.
