1. General Terms of Service

By accessing the Ranchi Pathology website or booking any diagnostic test or health package across our branches (Chandwa, Lohardaga, and Ranchi) or through Home Sample Collection, you agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions.

  • Diagnostic services are intended for medical screening and monitoring in consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.
  • You confirm that all patient details (full name, age, gender, phone number, and collection address) provided at booking are accurate and complete.
  • Ranchi Pathology reserves the right to update test pricing, package inclusions, branch timings, and promotional offers without prior notice; however, confirmed bookings retain the price locked at booking time.

2. Appointments & Sample Collection

  • Slot Availability: Appointments are subject to branch capacity and holiday calendars. Slots are confirmed upon submission and verification.
  • Fasting & Preparation: Certain tests (e.g., Fasting Blood Sugar, Lipid Profile, LFT, KFT) require 8–12 hours of fasting. Patients must follow preparation instructions; non-compliance may require rescheduling or affect test accuracy.
  • Home Collection: Our phlebotomist will arrive within the selected slot window. Please ensure accurate address, landmark, and reachable phone number. Applicable home collection charges (if any, depending on branch configuration) are displayed transparently prior to confirmation.
  • Repeat Sampling: In rare instances where a sample is hemolyzed, insufficient, or unsuitable for processing, we will notify you promptly to arrange a free repeat sample collection.

3. Pricing, Coupons & Payments

  • All prices are listed in Indian Rupees (INR).
  • Promotional coupon codes are validated server-side against minimum order value, validity dates, usage limits, and applicable tests/packages.
  • Payments can be made via UPI QR code generated for your booking reference or at the branch counter prior to sample processing.
  • Reports are released only after payment verification is completed.

4. Cancellation & Refund Policy

We strive to keep cancellations and rescheduling straightforward and fair for our patients:

  • Free Rescheduling: You may request to reschedule your branch visit or home collection slot at least 2 hours before the scheduled appointment time at no additional charge by contacting the respective branch.
  • Cancellation Before Sample Collection: Bookings cancelled prior to sample collection (or before a phlebotomist has been dispatched for home collection) are eligible for a 100% refund of any prepaid amount.
  • Cancellation After Dispatch / At Doorstep: If a home collection phlebotomist has already been dispatched or arrives at the address and the patient declines collection, any applicable home collection/visit fee may be deducted from the refund.
  • After Sample Processing Begins: Once a biological sample has been collected and laboratory processing has commenced, cancellations and refunds are not permitted for those processed tests.
  • Refund Timeline: Approved refunds are processed back to the original UPI/payment source within 5–7 business days.

5. Reports, Accuracy & Limitation of Liability

  • Estimated report turnaround times are indicative and may occasionally extend due to technical re-runs or quality verification protocols.
  • Reports are accessible online via your unique tracking token or Booking ID + registered phone verification. Please keep your tracking token confidential.
  • Pathology reports must be interpreted by a registered medical practitioner alongside clinical correlation. Ranchi Pathology is not liable for self-diagnosis or self-medication based on lab results.
  • For privacy practices regarding your medical records, please refer to our Privacy Policy.
